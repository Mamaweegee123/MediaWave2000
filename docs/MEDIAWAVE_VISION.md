MediaWave Vision
What is MediaWave?

MediaWave recreates the experience of browsing live cable television using your own media library.

Rather than organizing files into playlists, MediaWave turns folders into channels that continuously broadcast movies, shows, music, weather, and online content on a live schedule.

The goal is not to automate television stations.

The goal is to recreate the joy of flipping through channels.

Our Philosophy

MediaWave should feel:

welcoming
nostalgic
playful
relaxing
discoverable

Every screen should encourage exploration.

Users should feel like they are building their own television network, not configuring enterprise software.

Beginner First

A first-time user should be able to:

create channels
begin watching TV
understand what is happening

without reading documentation.

Advanced controls should exist...

...but they should never be the first thing someone sees.
